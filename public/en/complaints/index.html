Customer service channel

Complaints and Claims

Riego Central maintains a direct channel to receive complaints, observations or claims related to customer service, the website or a service.

Complaints channel

You can send your complaint or claim to:

reclamos@riegocentral.com

This channel is specifically intended for complaints and claims. For general inquiries or quote requests, use info@riegocentral.com or WhatsApp at +506 8631-0650.

Recommended information

To help identify and review the case, it is useful to include:

  • Name and contact information.
  • A clear description of what happened.
  • The related service or request.
  • The request tracking ID, if one was issued by the website form.
  • Approximate date of the interaction, when applicable.
  • Any additional information that helps identify the case.

There is no need to send sensitive information unrelated to the complaint.

Handling the complaint

The information received will be used to identify, review and respond to the case. Associated personal data will be processed in accordance with the Privacy Policy.

If the complaint relates to a request submitted through the website form, include the tracking ID received by email to make it easier to identify the case.

Receipt of an email does not by itself imply acceptance of responsibility or a favorable resolution; each case must be reviewed according to its circumstances.

Access to the channel

Riego Central does not charge for use of the complaints email channel. Any connection or Internet provider costs incurred by the user are not controlled by Riego Central.
