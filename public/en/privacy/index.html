Information and data protection

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Riego Central receives when a person uses the website form, email or contact channels, and how that information is used.

1. Responsible party

Riego Central is the trade name used to handle requests received through this website in Costa Rica.

General email: info@riegocentral.com Complaints channel: reclamos@riegocentral.com WhatsApp: +506 8631-0650

2. Information requested

The contact form requests only the information needed to understand and handle a service or quote request:

  • Name.
  • Phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Email address.
  • Service of interest.
  • Area where the service is needed.
  • Brief description of the need or project.

If a person voluntarily contacts Riego Central by email or WhatsApp, the information they choose to share through those channels is also processed.

3. Purpose of processing

The information is used to answer inquiries, review service needs, prepare or follow up on quotes, coordinate communications related to a request, and handle complaints or matters related to the service.

Riego Central does not use information from the form to sell databases or for advertising purposes unrelated to the original request.

4. Consent

Before submitting the form, the person must accept this Privacy Policy and authorize the processing of the information provided for the purpose of handling the request.

Personal data is processed taking into account the principles of informed consent and purpose established in Costa Rica Law No. 8968, Protection of the Person Regarding the Processing of Personal Data.

5. Service providers involved

Technology providers required to operate the website and deliver requests, such as Cloudflare and Resend, may participate in the process. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile as an anti-spam measure; this service may process technical signals required to determine whether an interaction appears legitimate. When a person uses WhatsApp, that channel is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and policies.

These services may process technical information or contact data to the extent necessary to provide their functions.

6. Retention

Information is retained only for the time reasonably necessary to handle the request, follow up on the commercial relationship and comply with obligations that may apply.

7. Rights of the data subject

The data subject may request information about their data, request correction when it is inaccurate, and request deletion when applicable, in accordance with applicable regulations.

To exercise these rights, write to info@riegocentral.com, clearly describing the request and providing the information needed to identify the related contact.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are applied to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. No system connected to the Internet can guarantee zero risk, so measures are reviewed as the website evolves.

9. Cookies and analytics

Riego Central has not currently incorporated advertising or analytics tools that require non-essential cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated and the corresponding mechanisms will be implemented.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when services, technology providers or applicable obligations change. The version published on this page will be the version in effect for the website.
